W 02 · Own project

Running coach

I ran for years without a coach. Borrowed plans, generic schedules, training blocks that never quite fit. When I got a bib for the 20 km of Brussels, I built what was missing.

A coach I can talk to. It knows my training, adjusts when a week goes sideways and keeps building toward race day. I use it every day, and it taught me what a small custom tool can do for one runner, and maybe one day for more.

Type
Self-initiated tool
Scope
Concept, design, build
Year
2026

WHAT I DO

  • A

    Websites

    Designed and built by the same person, end to end. From a single page to a full online shop. Quick on a phone, clear to move through, made to be found.

  • B

    Visual identity

    Logos, identities, vectorisation, print and vehicle graphics. A clear visual base, so everything you put out looks like it comes from the same place.

  • C

    Custom tools

    Booking and quote forms, automatic reminders, small apps made for the way you work. The repetitive parts of your week, taken off your hands.

How a project runs
  1. 01

    Listen

    A few questions and a conversation, so I understand the business before I draw anything.

  2. 02

    Agree

    A written summary of what you get, what it costs and when. Clear price, clear scope, no surprises.

  3. 03

    Make

    Early versions, fast. You see it take shape and we go back and forth until it's right.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Tested on every screen and handed over with a walkthrough. I stay reachable after.

HOW I SEE

A moodboard is how I think before I make anything. These are the images I return to. Not because they all belong to the same world, but because together they map the intersection I live in: running culture, craft objects, dark aesthetics, bodies in motion, cities at edge hours.

ABOUT

Designer first. Builder since I found out I could make the whole thing myself.

I studied Idea and Innovation Management, worked in trend research at Trendwatching and come from graphic design. After two years in Bangkok I'm back in Brussels, where I run a lot and work for myself.

I treat a project as if I'm part of your team. Plenty of back and forth, honest opinions, clear prices. I don't stop until we're both proud of it.
